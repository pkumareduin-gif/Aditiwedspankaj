PALACE VIDEO LIGHTING BLEND

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Keep index.html, palace-dark.png and palace-lighting.mp4 together in one folder.
3. Open index.html in Chrome.
4. Click the central diya (or the scene) once.
5. The supplied lighting video fades in over the dark still over 6.5 seconds and
   continues looping so the real flame motion remains visible.
6. Click Replay to return to the dark opening state.

Do not rename or separate the video and image files unless you also update their
paths in index.html. For web hosting, upload all three files to the same folder.
